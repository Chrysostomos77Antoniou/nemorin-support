Nemorin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Nemorin is developed by Chrysostomos Antoniou. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data stored on your device

Items you save (text, links, photos, voice notes and the text recognised from them) are stored only on your device. We do not run servers, require an account, or receive your content.

Permissions

Location (optional): used to remember where you were when you save an item and show the street and city, using Apple's on-device geocoding services. You can turn this off in Settings.

Weather (optional): if enabled, an approximate location is sent to the Open-Meteo weather service (open-meteo.com) to look up the weather at that moment. No account or identifier is sent by us.

Microphone and speech recognition: used only while you record a voice note, and the text is generated on your iPhone.

Photos and camera: used only when you choose to add a picture. Text in pictures is recognised on your device.

Tracking and third parties

Nemorin contains no advertising, analytics or tracking. We do not sell or share your data.

Deleting your data

Delete items inside the app, or delete the app to remove all its data from your device.

Children

Nemorin does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
